6. Earth Applications Spotlight (Space-to-Ground Spin-Offs)

🚒 Next-Gen Fine-Water-Mist & Clean Extinguishers (FLEX & BASS-II):
Droplet combustion physics in 0g directly improved ultra-fine water-mist suppression systems used in Earth server rooms, submarines, and aircraft cabins without toxic Halon gases.
🚨 False-Alarm-Proof Smoke Detectors (SAME & SPICE):
Mapping soot fractal aggregation in microgravity enabled multi-wavelength optical sensors on Earth that distinguish real toxic smoke from cooking steam or dust.
⚡ Low-Emission Engines & Cool-Flame Chemistry (CFI-G & ACME):
NASA's discovery of low-temperature "cool flame" chemistry in heptane droplets is now embedded in terrestrial HCCI automotive engine simulations to boost fuel efficiency and cut soot emissions.
